The first job is to find the dancers. The computer does not know where you are in the picture, so it checks the whole photo for something that looks like a head and a body, a bit like playing hide-and-seek or Where’s Waldo. When it finds a dancer, it draws a box around them. Then it cuts out that box and makes it bigger, like a zoom, so that the next step can look at the dancer closely. With two players, there are two boxes: player 1 always dances on the left side of the picture and player 2 on the right, and that is how the game knows who is who.
