STEP 3: CONNECT THE DOTS

Like the dot-to-dot pages of an activity book!

1. The stickers

2. Join them with lines

3. A stick figure!

Once the stickers are on, the rest is easy: join them with lines, exactly like a dot-to-dot page in an activity book. Shoulder to elbow, elbow to wrist, hip to knee, knee to ankle. What you get is a stick figure, which scientists call a skeleton, even though it has nothing to do with real bones. Because the stickers follow you in every new photo, the stick figure moves exactly like you. That is the same glowing kind of figure as the coach in the game.
