STEP 2: 33 MAGIC STICKERS

Inside the box, the computer puts a sticker on 33 special points of your body.

11 on your face: nose, eyes, ears, mouth

6 on your arms: shoulders, elbows, wrists

6 on your fingers

2 on your hips

8 on your legs: knees, ankles, heels, toes

How does it know where? It practised on thousands of photos where people had put the stickers by hand, just like you learned what a cat looks like by seeing lots of cats.

Now the computer looks inside the box and puts a sticker on 33 special points of the body. These points are called landmarks. Count them with the children: 11 on the face, 6 on the arms, 6 on the fingers, 2 on the hips and 8 on the legs and feet. That makes 33. How can a computer know where your elbow is? Nobody wrote a rule like “an elbow is a bend in the arm”. Instead, the program practised on many thousands of photos where people had placed the stickers by hand. After seeing enough examples, it can place the stickers on a photo it has never seen, a bit like how you can recognise a cat you have never met because you have seen lots of cats before.
