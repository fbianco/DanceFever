Big or small, near or far: only the direction counts. The game checks 9 arrows: 4 in your arms, 4 in your legs, 1 for your back.

Now the game compares your stick figure with the coach’s. Each piece of your arm or leg is like an arrow, or like the hand of a clock. The white dashed arrow is the coach’s. If your arrow points the same way, that is PERFECT. If it is a little bit off, that is GOOD. If it points somewhere else, it is a MISS. The game only looks at which way the arrows point, not how long they are or where you stand. So a small child and a tall grown-up doing the same move get the same score. It checks 9 arrows: upper arms and forearms, thighs and shins, and your back. Some moves do not use the legs, and then only the arms and the back count.
