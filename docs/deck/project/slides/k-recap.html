Let’s put it all together. The webcam takes a photo made of half a million coloured dots. The computer finds each dancer and draws a box. It puts 33 stickers on the body. It joins them into a stick figure. And it checks whether your arrows point the same way as the coach’s. If they do: points, confetti and PERFECT! The amazing part is the speed: the webcam sends 30 photos every second, and the computer does all five steps for every photo. A blink of the eye takes about a tenth of a second, and in that time the game has already done everything three times. End of part 1. Part 2 tells the same story for grown-ups who like equations.
