HOW DOES THE COMPUTER SEE YOU DANCE?

From a webcam picture to a dancing stick figure, in 33 milliseconds

Part 1 · for dancers aged 6 to 10

Part 2 · for mathematicians and physicists

One or two players copy the glowing coach. The webcam checks every move.

Dance Fever is a dance game in the style of Just Dance: one or two players stand in front of a webcam and copy the moves of a glowing coach. The game gives points for every move you match. There is no controller: the only input is the camera picture. This deck answers one question: how does the computer turn a camera picture into a skeleton it can compare with the coach? Part 1 tells it for children aged 6 to 10. Part 2 tells exactly the same story again, with the mathematics and physics.
